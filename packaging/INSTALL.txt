Rotor Rescue for FrSky ETHOS

Use an offline model with both RF modules disabled before using the sticks
in the game. You can also play in the X20RS simulator in FrSky Suite.

Installation in FrSky Suite
1. Open Lua library > Install Lua Scripts and select the Rotor Rescue ZIP.
2. Install the script, disconnect the radio safely and restart ETHOS.
3. Open System and find Rotor Rescue. Swipe to the next page if needed.

Manual installation
Extract main.lua, icon.png and the sounds folder with all its WAV files
into the scripts/RotorRescue folder on the radio. Restart ETHOS. When
updating manually, remove that folder's stale main.luac and the old
rotor*.wav and shift*.wav files before restart.

Flying
The game fills the whole screen. Tap the < button at the top left or press
RTN to leave it.
Lower throttle fully. Raise it gently to spool up and take off. About 52%
power hovers with a level rotor; start at roughly 60% to lift. On Mode 2,
throttle is left stick vertical and aileron is right stick horizontal.
Use small aileron inputs and countersteer before reaching the target.
Keep the skids inside the highlighted pad, level and moving slowly.
Land on RIDGE, LOOKOUT and CAMP in order. Stay down for 1.6 seconds at each
pad to pick up both passengers and score 100, 200 and 300 points. If you
leave early, they wait and wave until you land again. After CAMP, climb
over LOOKOUT, then land at BASE with all six passengers to finish.

Use the colored approach cues to correct your height, speed and tilt.
After a crash, Retry returns to the last pickup with passengers and points.
New Run clears the current trip. The timer starts at first liftoff.
Delivery gives 300 more points. The bonus starts at 600 and decreases by
4 points per second and 150 per retry, down to zero. TOP SCORES shows the
five best completed trips on this radio. Tap the sound control to mute.
The radio vibrates on a crash, on each pickup and when the mission is
complete; the strength follows the haptic setting under System. Tap the
version line under the title to show frames per second and paint time.
