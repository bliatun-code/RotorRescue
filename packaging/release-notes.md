# Rotor Rescue 0.7.2 — ENTER keeps working

- ENTER now acts when the key is pressed. Retry, Fly Again and closing Top Scores with ENTER keep working after a touch outside the buttons or after one RTN press.
- Flying, sound and scoring are the same as in 0.7.1.

## About Rotor Rescue

- Fly a two-stick helicopter in the System menu and rescue six passengers from three platforms.
- Hold each landing for 1.6 seconds and fly all passengers back to Base.
- Compare your five best finished flights by points, time and retries.
- Play in the ETHOS simulator or use an offline model with RF disabled on the radio.
